## 🚀 Overview
Mocha Pro for Windows is a professional planar tracking toolset that installs as a plugin inside Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro and other compatible hosts, and it also ships as a standalone application. Instead of tracking single points, Boris FX Mocha Pro follows flat planes and surfaces across a shot, giving artists dependable data for rotoscoping, screen inserts, object removal, and match move. Editors and compositors reach for the Mocha Pro plugin when they need clean masks, stabilized shots, and seamless removals without leaving their timeline. Because the panel opens directly from the host's effects menu, the tracking and roto work stays part of the same project you are already editing.

## ✨ Features
- **Planar tracking** — Track flat surfaces and regions with sub-pixel accuracy for reliable inserts, corner-pins, and stabilization.
- **Object removal and clean plate** — Paint out rigs, logos, and unwanted elements using tracked reference frames for natural results.
- **Advanced rotoscoping** — Draw editable spline masks that follow motion, cutting isolation time on complex shots.
- **Mesh warp and stabilize** — Fix warped surfaces, remove jitter, and lock down handheld footage inside the host.
- **Host integration** — Apply Mocha Pro as an effect in After Effects, Premiere Pro, and other supported applications, keeping renders in your existing pipeline.

## 📥 Installation
1. Click the **Download** button above to get the latest version of the Mocha Pro installer for Windows.
2. Run the downloaded installer and follow the on-screen setup steps, choosing the plugin versions for your installed hosts.
3. Activate your license by signing in to your Boris FX account or entering your license details when prompted.
4. Open Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, or another supported host — Mocha Pro appears in the Effects menu, ready to apply to a layer or clip.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open your host application and load the project and shot you want to work on.
2. Add the Mocha Pro effect to your layer or clip from the host's Effects menu.
3. Open the Mocha interface and draw a spline around the surface or object you want to track.
4. Run the planar tracker, then use the tracked data for masking, inserts, removal, or stabilization.
5. Return to the host timeline and render your shot as part of your normal workflow.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is Mocha Pro free?**
Mocha Pro is a paid product from Boris FX, but a free trial is available so you can evaluate the planar tracking and object removal tools before you buy a license.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
Mocha Pro runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11. It also depends on a supported version of your chosen host, such as After Effects or Premiere Pro.

**Is my data secure?**
Your footage and project files stay on your own machine. Mocha Pro processes media locally inside your host application, and license activation is handled through your Boris FX account.
